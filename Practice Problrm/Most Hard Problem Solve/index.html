Example Walkthrough

Input: num = "1234", t = 256
Step 1: Prime Factorization of t
\( t = 256 = 2^8 \)
We need 8 factors of 2.
Step 2: Minimum Digits for the Product
Each digit contributes to the prime factors:
- \( 8 = 2^3 \), so two 8s give \( 2^6 \).
- \( 4 = 2^2 \), completes \( 2^8 \).
Minimum digits: [8, 8].
Step 3: Traverse num
Replace digits to construct the smallest valid number:
- First digit: Keep 1.
- Second digit: Replace 2 → 4.
- Third digit: Replace 3 → 8.
- Fourth digit: Replace 4 → 8.
Resulting number: 1488
Step 4: Verify Product
Calculate product of digits in 1488:
\( 1 \times 4 \times 8 \times 8 = 256 \).
Since 256 is divisible by \( t = 256 \), this is valid.
Output: "1488"
